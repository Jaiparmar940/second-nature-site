What we build

Training environments and evals for AI that acts in the physical world.

AI labs and robotics teams bring us a real workflow, its failure traces, and the outcomes that matter. We turn them into an interactive simulator where an agent observes through sensors and instruments, chooses actions, and experiences their physical consequences.

The result is an RL-ready training ground and a cheat-resistant evaluation: agents practice decisions and recovery under time, cost, and safety constraints, then earn a score based on what actually happened instead of how plausible their explanation sounded.

The platform

Training environments and test rigs for physical reasoning.

01 / PHYSICS

Physics, not scripted answers

Agents interact with a live system model, not a tree of hand-authored responses. State, measurements, and consequences are computed from the governing physics, so each task can vary without breaking the rules of the machine.

02 / GRADING

Outcomes, not plausible text

Graders reward correct causal understanding, disciplined action, and efficient goal completion against an expert baseline. The environment state determines whether an action worked; confident explanations and brute-force guessing do not.

03 / VERIFICATION

Expert verification is the moat

Models can generate simulations they cannot warrant. Coding agents produce much of the implementation scaffolding, scenario variants, and automated tests; human expertise is concentrated on verifying system dynamics, tasks, and rewards. In no-start-env, that review caught four physics errors that passed the automated suite, and each became an invariant that checks later scenarios.
